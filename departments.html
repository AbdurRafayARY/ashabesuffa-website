Our Departments

Comprehensive programs for Islamic learning and community development.

📖

Hifz & Tajweed

Our Hifz program is designed for students who wish to memorize the Holy Quran with proper Tajweed. Experienced teachers guide students through a structured curriculum, ensuring correct pronunciation and understanding.

  • ✅ Full-time and part-time options
  • ✅ Individual attention
  • ✅ Tajweed rules and practice
  • ✅ Annual competitions
🕌

Dars-e-Nizami

The Dars-e-Nizami is an eight-year comprehensive Islamic scholarship program covering Arabic grammar, literature, Fiqh, Hadith, Tafsir, and Islamic history. Graduates receive a recognized degree equivalent to M.A. in Islamic Studies.

  • ✅ Eight-year structured curriculum
  • ✅ Qualified scholars as teachers
  • ✅ Specialization in Hadith and Fiqh
  • ✅ Recognized certification
🎓

Primary Education

Our primary education department provides a balanced curriculum that combines modern subjects with Islamic education. We focus on building a strong foundation in both academics and character.

  • ✅ Modern curriculum up to Matric
  • ✅ Islamic studies and Quran
  • ✅ Experienced teaching staff
  • ✅ Co-curricular activities
🤝

Welfare & Dawah

Our welfare department serves the community through various initiatives, including food distribution, orphan support, medical assistance, and Islamic outreach programs.

  • ✅ Monthly food packages
  • ✅ Orphan sponsorship
  • ✅ Medical camps
  • ✅ Dawah and counseling

About Us

Learn about our mission, vision, and the work we do.

Our Story

Ashabesuffa Foundation was established with a simple yet powerful vision: to spread the light of Islamic knowledge and serve humanity. Over the years, we have grown into a comprehensive institution offering Quranic education, traditional Islamic scholarship, and social welfare programs.

Our foundation is built on the principles of the Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama'ah, following the moderate and balanced approach of the righteous predecessors. We strive to produce graduates who are not only knowledgeable but also exemplary in character and dedicated to serving their communities.

Our Vision

To be a leading Islamic educational institution that nurtures faith, knowledge, and service, contributing to a just and compassionate society.

Our Values

  • ✅ Excellence in education
  • ✅ Compassion and service to humanity
  • ✅ Integrity and transparency
  • ✅ Community engagement
